Housing Tips & AF Bostäder

Securing student housing in Lund is competitive. Understand novisch lottery, student corridors, and how to verify if private landlords are legitimate to avoid common scams.

✈️

Arrival Day Logistics

Landing at Copenhagen Airport (CPH) is the most popular route. Learn how to purchase Skånetrafiken tickets to Lund C, where the registration desk is, and how to collect keys.

♻️

Swedish Pant System

Don't throw away bottles and cans! Sweden recycles using the 'Pant' reverse vending machines at grocery stores. Get 1–2 SEK back per bottle for your grocery budget.

🗑️

Waste Sorting & Recycling

Swedes take waste sorting seriously. Learn the difference between food waste (matavfall), paper, plastic, metal, and residual waste (restavfall) to avoid fines or landlord disputes.

🚌

Public Transit (Skånetrafiken)

Getting around Lund and Skåne is easy with green city buses and regional trains. Get the Skånetrafiken app, apply for student discounts, and learn tap-on rules.

🧺

Laundry Booking ("Tvättstuga")

Shared laundry rooms are highly organized in Swedish housing. Learn how to book using digital key tags or locks, the strict cleanup rules, and general tvättstuga etiquette.
